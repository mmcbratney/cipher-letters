# Cipher Letters

Write-ups of cipher letters (1537-1885) worked on in October and November 2026 by Malcolm McBratney (Brisbane) working with Claude (Anthropic), each first filed in [Cyphersolver](https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver): fifteen checked and merged, four submitted and under review (marked so on their pages). Last updated 27 Nov 2026. The Ridolfi letter of 1646 is Brice Dudout's reading, written up with his credit first.

Site: https://mmcbratney.github.io/cipher-letters/

Every figure comes from the merged Cyphersolver files, or from the open pull request for pages marked under review. No archive images are copied; pages link to the archives' records. Text CC BY 4.0.
