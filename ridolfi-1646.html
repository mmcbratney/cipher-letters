Five hundred horse and a refused passage: Ludovico Ridolfi to the duque de Arcos, 8 May 1646

This letter was read in part by Brice Dudout, working with AI agents. His full account is on Europe chiffrée: Rome, 1646. This page is a short note.

What is new and what was known

No decipherment of the time and no edition was found. The reading is Brice Dudout's: see his article.

The letter

Five pages wholly in cipher except the date, "Di Roma li 8 Maggio 1646".

What it says

The Pope has refused Don Antonio Ronquillo passage for five hundred horse the viceroy is sending to help the Grand Duke of Tuscany, but would grant it towards Milan. Ridolfi advises accepting.

"Gia che questa mattina i[l] Papa ha negato al Signor Don Antonio Ronchillo / il paso per li {cinqueceto} caualli""This morning the Pope refused Signor Don Antonio Ronquillo passage for the five hundred horse"

The cipher

Codes 20-44 are letters (vowels twice), syllables run in series of five, and three-digit groups are a nomenclator.

How it was read

Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic) proposed the key in one run on pages 1-4 alone; page 5 was held out.

Checks

Held-out page 5, one measure fixed in advance: 0.817 of decoded letters covered by Italian words of four or more letters, against at most 0.487 for 200 shuffled keys. Brice Dudout re-read 19 doubtful digits blind on the images: 12 corrections confirmed.

How much is read?

396 of 431 words = 91.9% by the read bar. Brice Dudout's own count, which takes 21 code values given by the sense as read, is 417 of 431 = 96.8%.

What remains uncertain

Five nomenclator codes (103, 110, 115, 116, 136) are open and 9 words are restored.

Who did what

Brice Dudout chose the letter and set the rules; Claude Fable 5.2 proposed the key and read pages 1-4; his Claude Opus 5.5 agents transcribed, measured page 5, read it and wrote up (PR #403); he re-read 19 digits blind on the images himself (PR #406). Daniel Bourdeau's PARES sweep listed it (catalogue 748).

Malcolm McBratney (Brisbane), working with Claude, reported his own recovery of the same letter in issue #421 (12 Nov 2026): his own transcription of the images and his own key from the ciphertext alone, made by his record on 11 Nov 2026 without knowledge of PR #403, sent as corroboration, not to dispute credit. All 71 of his letter and syllable values match Brice Dudout's key; 1,393 of 1,412 tokens agree. His timeline cannot be checked from the repository.

AI agents transcribed, solved and read the letter. Brice Dudout re-read 19 digits on the images; otherwise no person checked the transcription or the reading, and no palaeographer.
