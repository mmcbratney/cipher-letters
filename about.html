About

Cipher Letters is a set of write-ups of early modern cipher letters read in October and November 2026, all but one by Malcolm McBratney, a lawyer in Brisbane, working with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The Ridolfi letter of 1646 is Brice Dudout's reading, written up here with his credit first.

The letters run from 1537 to 1727: Italian court and diplomatic letters in Modena, Florence and Toledo, Danish and Swedish diplomatic correspondence in Copenhagen and Stockholm, an Irish father's cipher test for his son, and a German alchemical notebook in London.

Each letter was first filed, with all its files, in Cyphersolver, Daniel Bourdeau's open catalogue and workbench for unsolved historical ciphers, where it was checked and merged. These pages tell the story of each letter for a general reader and link back to that record.
