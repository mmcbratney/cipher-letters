About

Cipher Letters is a set of write-ups of cipher letters, mostly early modern, worked on in October and November 2026, all but one by Malcolm McBratney, a lawyer in Brisbane, working with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The Ridolfi letter of 1646 is Brice Dudout's reading, written up here with his credit first.

The letters run from 1537 to 1737: Italian court and diplomatic letters in Modena, Florence and Toledo, Danish and Swedish diplomatic correspondence in Copenhagen and Stockholm, an Irish father's cipher test for his son, a Russian rescript to the resident in Persia, and a German alchemical notebook in London. Four more, submitted and still under review, add an Este letter of 1547, the Dutch East India Company's number cipher of 1623-1643, a Fenian letter of 1885 and a seventeenth-century Key of Solomon in Basel (decoded, not read).

Each letter was first filed, with all its files, in Cyphersolver, Daniel Bourdeau's open catalogue and workbench for unsolved historical ciphers, where it was checked and merged, or, for the pages and parts marked "under review", is still waiting for that check. These pages tell the story of each letter for a general reader and link back to that record.
